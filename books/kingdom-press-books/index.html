The Jubilee Kingdom

A Story of Freedom, Restoration, and the Reign of Grace

Across seven years and seven cycles, a divided land burdened by debt, exile and forgotten promises watches a hidden Kingdom begin to rise. It comes not through conquest, but through forgiveness, release and restoration.

The author

Duane C. McDermott

Duane C. McDermott is a writer and visual storyteller whose work blends memoir, cinematic imagery, street history, faith and ambition.

Through Kingdom Press, he tells stories of restoration and purpose, from biblical history to the neighbourhoods of Houston, with artwork on every page.
